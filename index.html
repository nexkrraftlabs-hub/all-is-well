Frequently Asked Questions

Your first visit includes a comprehensive physical evaluation by Dr. Swati or Dr. Akhilesh. We discuss your pain history, movement limits, review any MRI/X-ray scans, and design an immediate custom treatment plan.

No formal referral is needed. You can book an appointment directly or walk in. If you have previous medical records or imaging, please bring them along.

We treat slip disc (PIVD), sciatica, cervical spondylosis, sports injuries, frozen shoulder, ACL rehabilitation, arthritis, stroke recovery, and post-operative joint rehabilitation.

Most patients experience significant pain reduction within the first few sessions thanks to our targeted combination of manual therapy, electro-modalities, and tailored corrective exercise.

Our clinic is located Near Saisoveer Mandir, Manas Nagar, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. You can call us directly at +91 79853 81733 or 07942700816 for directions or booking an appointment. We are open Monday to Saturday until 9:00 PM.
