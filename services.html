Neurological Physiotherapy

The World Confederation for Physical Therapy (WCPT) and the Australian Physiotherapy Association recognize Neurology and Neuroscience as a vital medical subgroup. Neurological physiotherapy focuses on assessing and treating individuals who have movement and physical function disorders resulting from impairments in the body’s neuromuscular and central nervous systems.

Led by Dr. Swati Saxena (MPT Neurology, Gold Medalist), our clinic provides intensive neuroplasticity retraining for:

  • Stroke & Hemiplegia Recovery (Gait and upper limb rehabilitation)
  • Paralysis, Hemiparesis & Facial Palsy (Bell's Palsy)
  • Parkinson’s Disease & Movement Impairments
  • Peripheral Nerve Injuries & Spinal Cord Rehabilitation
Non-Surgical Spine Decompression

Spinal Physiotherapy

Spinal physiotherapy is a sub-specialty discipline of musculoskeletal physiotherapy that employs specialized physical interventions such as manual therapy, computerized mechanical traction, postural realignment, and core strengthening exercises to resolve acute and chronic spinal disorders.

Led by Dr. Akhilesh Gupta (MPT Musculoskeletal), our spinal protocols effectively relieve:

  • Slip Disc (PIVD – Prolapsed Intervertebral Disc)
  • Sciatica & Lumbar Radiculopathy (Leg nerve pain)
  • Cervical Spondylosis, Neck Pain & Upper Arm Radiating Numbness
  • Ankylosing Spondylitis & Chronic Degenerative Disc Disease
Hands-On Clinical Skills

Manual Therapy

Manual therapy is hands-on therapy utilizing multiple proven manual techniques. According to the American Physical Therapy Association (APTA): “Manual therapy techniques are skilled hand movements intended to produce any or all of the following effects: improve tissue extensibility; increase range of motion of the joint complex; mobilize or manipulate soft tissues and joints; induce relaxation; change muscle function; modulate pain; and reduce soft tissue swelling, inflammation or movement restriction.”

  • Joint Mobilization (Maitland, Mulligan concepts)
  • Myofascial Trigger Point Release & Soft Tissue Friction
  • Spinal Facet Joint Decompression
  • Muscle Energy Techniques (MET) & Therapeutic Stretching

Pediatric Physiotherapy

Orthopedic & Joint Rehabilitation

Sports & Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy

Sports and musculoskeletal physiotherapy deals with injuries and structural impairments related to active individuals and degenerative joint diseases. It involves the prevention, acute management, and complete recovery from physical and outdoor exertion injuries.

  • Frozen Shoulder (Adhesive Capsulitis) Mobilization
  • Knee Osteoarthritis & Post-TKR (Total Knee Replacement) Rehabilitation
  • Tennis Elbow, Golfer's Elbow & Plantar Fasciitis (Heel Pain)
  • Ligament Sprains (ACL/MCL) and Meniscal Tears
