Appointment & Treatment Terms

Welcome to All Is Well Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation Centre. These Terms of Care apply to all appointments, consultations, and treatments provided by Dr. Swati and Dr. Akhilesh at our clinic in Manas Nagar, Lucknow.

1. Appointments

Appointments can be booked by phone, WhatsApp, or through our website contact form. We recommend booking in advance to secure your preferred time slot.

  • Clinic hours: Monday to Saturday, until 9:00 PM
  • Sunday: Call for appointment availability
  • Phone: +91 79853 81733 or 07942700816

2. Cancellation & Rescheduling

If you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment, please inform us at least 24 hours in advance so we can offer the slot to another patient.

3. First Visit

For your first visit, please bring:

  • Recent X-rays, MRI scans, or doctor referral slips (if any)
  • A list of current medications
  • Comfortable, loose clothing for joint assessment

4. Treatment & Fees

Treatment plans are tailored to each patient by Dr. Swati and Dr. Akhilesh. Session duration is typically 45 to 60 minutes. Fees vary based on the treatment plan and will be discussed during your consultation.

5. Patient Responsibility

Patients are responsible for following the prescribed treatment plan, attending scheduled sessions, and informing the clinic of any changes in their condition.

6. Limitation of Liability

All Is Well Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation Centre provides professional physiotherapy care. Individual results may vary based on the patient's condition, adherence to treatment, and other factors beyond our control.
